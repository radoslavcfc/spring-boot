# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people, regardless of their background, identity, or experience level.

We pledge that everyone participating in the spring-boot-repo community will:
- Be treated with respect and dignity
- Have a safe, harassment-free experience
- Be valued for their contributions
- Have their concerns addressed promptly and fairly

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of positive behavior that contributes to our community include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Providing helpful feedback and support
- Crediting others for their contributions

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, disability, or appearance
- Unwelcome sexual advances or attention
- Personal attacks or insults
- Publishing private information without consent
- Trolling, spamming, or disruptive behavior
- Any form of violence or threats
- Exclusionary behavior

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:
1. Report to [conduct@example.com](mailto:conduct@example.com)
2. Include detailed description and context
3. Provide evidence if possible
4. Keep communication confidential

### Investigation

- Reports are taken seriously
- Investigation conducted promptly and fairly
- Confidentiality of reporters protected
- All parties given opportunity to respond

### Action

Consequences for violations may include:
- Warning
- Temporary muting or suspension
- Permanent removal from community
- Legal action if warranted

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- GitHub repositories and discussions
- Issue comments and pull requests
- Commit messages
- Community forums and chat
- In-person community events
- Any interaction representing the project
